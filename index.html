Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can't find your answer? Call (813) 860-1069 and talk to us directly.

What areas does Moulton's Heating & Cooling serve?

We serve homes and businesses throughout the Tampa Bay area of Florida and the surrounding areas. If you're not sure whether we cover your address, give us a call at (813) 860-1069.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. We install HVAC systems for both residential and commercial properties, including rooftop units on commercial buildings.

Why is routine HVAC maintenance worth it?

Routine maintenance keeps your system running efficiently and helps prevent costly breakdowns, which matters in Tampa Bay where the AC works hard most of the year.

What does dryer vent cleaning do?

Cleaning out a dryer vent improves the air quality in your home and reduces the fire hazard that lint build-up creates.

Do you only work on heating and cooling?

No. Alongside HVAC we provide water heater service, including tank and tankless units, dryer vent cleaning and minor home repairs.

How do I get a quote?
