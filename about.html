About us

A Tampa Bay HVAC team that treats your home like it matters.

Moulton's Heating & Cooling delivers heating, ventilation and air conditioning service, dryer vent cleaning, water heater service and minor home repairs across Tampa Bay, with professionalism and care on every visit.

Who we are

Known for quality, built on trust.

Moulton's Heating & Cooling is a trusted HVAC service provider in the Tampa Bay area and its surrounding towns. We're known for exceptional quality in heating, ventilation and air conditioning, as well as dryer vent cleaning, water heater service and minor home repairs.

Letting someone into your home, especially when the AC has quit in the Florida heat, takes trust. That's why we focus on professionalism, respect and a commitment to exceeding your expectations from the moment we step through the door.

Routine maintenance, a repair or a complete HVAC installation: whatever you need, we're here to keep you comfortable year-round.

“Our dedicated team goes above and beyond to ensure your home's comfort and safety.”

What we stand for

Three promises on every visit.

  • Professionalism

    We show up prepared, explain the work in plain language and hold every job, large or small, to the same standard.

  • Respect

    Your home is not a job site. We treat it, and you, with care from arrival until the system is running again.

  • Going beyond

    We aim to exceed your expectations, whether that's spotting a problem early or handling the small repair while we're there.

What we work on

Homes, businesses and everything in between.

Our work covers residential and commercial properties: new system installs, routine maintenance, fast repairs, and the jobs other HVAC companies skip.

  • Heating & air conditioningInstallation, maintenance and repair.
  • Commercial HVACIncluding rooftop units set by crane.
  • Water heatersTank and tankless service and installs.
  • Dryer vent cleaning & minor repairsFor better air and a safer home.
